About

About me

Advancing inclusive and community-centred tourism through interdisciplinary research, higher education and global collaboration.

Dr. Junaid K.C. is a tourism researcher, anthropologist and educator. He is Head and Assistant Professor in the PG Department of Tourism & Management at Naipunnya School of Management, Cherthala, Kerala (affiliated to the University of Kerala).

He holds a Ph.D. in Tourism and Hospitality Management from H.N.B. Garhwal Central University, where his doctoral research examined tourism and hospitality education and training in the Malabar region of Kerala. Alongside the thesis he carried out field studies across Uttarakhand and the wider Indian Himalayan Region, with a focus on sustainable mountain tourism. His MBA in Tourism and MA in Anthropology were completed at Pondicherry Central University, and his B.Com is in Finance.

His work spans accessible and inclusive tourism, Himalayan and mountain tourism, sustainable and community-based tourism, tourism education and training, livelihoods, and the anthropology of tourism. He is Managing Editor of the Himalayan Tourism magazine, editor of volumes with IGI Global, Apple Academic Press (Taylor & Francis Group) and Emerald Publishing, and a National Fellow of the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India (December 2020). His accessible-tourism work is connected with PUPUZO (United for Rural and Inclusive Tourism).

I traveled alone throughout India, immersing myself in the vibrant villages of mountains, deserts, seashores, and islands. My journeys are to observe people and experience the richness of reality.

Research

Research interests

My research sits at the meeting point of tourism studies and anthropology. It looks at how destinations, communities and institutions can make tourism more inclusive, sustainable and genuinely beneficial for the people who live there, using ethnographic, qualitative and quantitative approaches.

Accessible Tourism

Advancing accessible tourism systems that enable full and equal participation in travel and leisure for persons with disabilities, older persons and people with diverse access needs, aligned with human rights, sustainability and universal design. Connected with PUPUZO (United for Rural and Inclusive Tourism).

  • Inclusive tourism
  • Universal design
  • PUPUZO

Himalayan & Mountain Tourism

Field studies across Uttarakhand and the Indian Himalayan Region, a systematic review of 1,036 studies (1977 to August 2023) on tourism research in the region, and the edited volume Himalayan Mountain Tourism: Toward Sustainable Adventure Tourism (2026).

  • Uttarakhand
  • Adventure tourism
  • Local governance

Sustainable Tourism

Thematic analysis of four decades of sustainability research in Himalayan tourism and hospitality, responsible tourism, and the forthcoming edited volume on climate change and human–wildlife conflict in the Indian Himalayan Region.

  • Responsible tourism
  • Climate change
  • Blue economy

Community-Based Tourism

Community-centred and community-driven approaches to tourism planning, including homestays in Uttarakhand, local governance in Himalayan adventure tourism and stakeholder participation in destination development.

  • Homestays
  • Participation
  • Local institutions

Tourism Education & Training

Doctoral research on tourism and hospitality education and training in the Malabar region of Kerala, with studies on entrepreneurship education in postgraduate tourism programmes, Skill India in hospitality and learner outcomes. Editor of Entrepreneurship Education in Tourism and Hospitality Management (IGI Global, 2022).

  • HRD
  • Entrepreneurship education
  • Skill India

Tourism & Livelihood Generation

Studies of homestays in Uttarakhand, employee-to-entrepreneur transitions and financing of SMEs in Indian tourism, and social entrepreneurship in ecotourism.

  • Tourism entrepreneurship
  • SMEs
  • Homestays

Anthropology & Tourism

Ethnographic and netnographic research, including an ethnographic profile of the Adiya tribe of Wayanad, slum tourism in Dharavi, drug tourism, voluntourism and netnography as a research method in India.

  • Ethnography
  • Netnography
  • Cultural change

Methods and fieldwork

Ethnography & netnography

Participant observation, in-depth interviews and online ethnography in tribal, destination and digital settings.

Systematic reviews & thematic analysis

Large-scale literature reviews across Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar and The Lens.

Content analysis

Syllabus and document analysis of tourism programmes using Atlas.ti.

Survey research & SEM

Primary surveys analysed with IBM SPSS and SPSS Amos structural equation modelling.
